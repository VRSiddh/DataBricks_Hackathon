Nyaya-Sahayak

न्याय सहायक · BNS-aware legal assistant

One interface for everyone — from a first-time complainant to a busy practitioner. Upload a document, pick your language, and get guidance grounded in the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS) corpus (via Databricks Vector Search when configured).

Grounded answers

RAG over your BNS chunks with similarity search, plus IPC→BNS hints where available.

Indic-first

Sarvam Mayura + Bulbul (when API keys are set) for translation and TTS.

Honest by design

The model is instructed to refuse when the text is unusable or off-topic for Indian criminal law.
